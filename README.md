
# HR Attrition Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing attrition across 1,000
employees to find who leaves and why.



![HR ATTRITION DASHBOAD](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2ddd7885-452d-4a52-a344-864edcb12530)




## Tools
Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX

## What I did
- Loaded and typed the data in Power Query
- Created helper columns: Attrition Flag, Tenure Band, Age Group, Salary Band
- Wrote DAX measures: Total Employees, Employees Left, Attrition Rate %,
  Avg Salary, Avg Tenure, Avg Satisfaction, Overtime %
- Built KPI cards, charts and slicers for Department, Job Level and Gender

## Key insights
- Attrition rate is 17.3% (173 of 1,000 employees)
- Overtime employees leave at 30.2% vs 12.2% for others
- Sales has the highest attrition (24.5%)
- Low satisfaction (scores 1-2) means 23-25% attrition
- Highest risk in the first 3 years of tenure
- Chennai (24.0%) and Mumbai (20.6%) are the highest-attrition cities
